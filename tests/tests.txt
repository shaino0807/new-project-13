## Test 15 - Macro hard rules and confidence policy are deterministic
Viewport: desktop and API contract inspection
Description: Verifies the formerly ambiguous VIX, A/D, focus, BofA, and public-action rules cannot drift between scans.

Steps:
1. Inspect a VIX observation with two distinct consecutive source trading dates above 25, then repeat with only one date above 25 and with two same-day snapshots.
2. Inspect an A/D sequence where S&P 500 makes a 60-session closing high while A/D is at least 1% below its 60-session high; test one, two, and three consecutive qualifying sessions.
3. Inspect focus selection with at least 253 daily observations, 53 weekly observations, and 37 monthly observations. Then repeat with shorter histories.
4. Inspect a BofA observation marked A, B, C, and D. For C, test one source, two independent current-week sources agreeing within 0.1, and two sources outside that tolerance.
5. Inspect the macro-risk page table, trigger cards, focus section, and limitations section.
6. Inspect `docs/macro-risk-data-sources-todo.md` without enabling credentials or purchasing any source.

Expected: VIX hard-trigger status is true only after two different consecutive source trading-session closes above 25, never because a user reran the same-day scan. A/D hard-trigger status is true only after all of: 60-session S&P closing high, A/D at least 1% below its own 60-session closing high, and three consecutive qualifying sessions; one or two sessions are at most near. Focus uses absolute one-period historical Z-score and selects the largest score only when it is at least 2.0; otherwise it reports the nearest normalized fixed threshold and its fallback method. BofA accepts A/B, or only two qualifying C sources, and all other evidence remains unknown. The public page does not state a fixed sell percentage: three of seven triggers starts a staged risk-reduction review. The purchase TODO lists NAAIM, AAII, GuruFocus, and BofA with licensing, data-quality, and regression gates, while no paid credential is configured.
